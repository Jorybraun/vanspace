# Orbit · Vancouver — voice

## Who speaks

**Orbit** is the event. It is not Devin’s product marketing page, and it is not a sci-fi movie trailer.

Address builders as **you**. The event is **Orbit** (or **Orbit · Vancouver**). Sponsors are named plainly.

## Tone

- Direct, calm, a little dry.
- Short sentences. Periods on fragments.
- Cosmic metaphor: **one per block max**. Earn it.
- No emoji. Almost no exclamation marks.
- No “supercharge,” “revolutionary,” or “excited to announce.”

## Casing

| Surface | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Headlines | Sentence case |
| Eyebrows | UPPERCASE mono |
| Buttons | Sentence case — `Get tickets` (primary). No public “Buy sponsorship” CTA with prices. |
| Schedule times | `09:30` 24h or `9:30 AM` — pick one and keep it |

## Good

> A micro-conference for builders under the stars.  
> In a gigantic saucer — at the H.R. MacMillan Space Centre.  
> Tickets are limited because the room is.

## Bad

> 🚀 Supercharge your AI journey at the MOST EPIC space conference!!!  
> We’re thrilled to partner with amazing brands…  
> Join us for a day of inspiration and networking opportunities.

## Sponsor language

- **Tier language:** Platinum / Gold / Silver / Bronze — never “the Devin package”
- **On site after sale:** “Platinum sponsor — [Company]” + their lockup
- Supporting: “Sponsors” not “partners transforming the ecosystem”
- Never recolor sponsor marks (incl. Devin) to mist purple

## Mission Control (agent surface)

If the agent speaks, it is **terse, operational**, like a ground controller:

> Doors at 09:00. Keynote at 10:00 in the main hall.  
> Need the schedule for agent talks? Three sessions after lunch.

Not:

> Hey friend!! Ready for an out-of-this-world adventure? 🌌
