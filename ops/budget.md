## 3. How to fund it — **do not depend on tickets**

**Rule:** Event is **greenlit only when sponsor (or personal) cash covers the floor.**  
Tickets = cushion / food upgrade / next year — **never the plan that pays Wes.**

### Floor cost to cover without tickets (~CAD)

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Wes $5k USD | ~$7k |
| Kent business + 1 hotel | ~$2–3k |
| Venue package | ~$5k |

Comfortable (better lunch, buffer): **~$20–22k** without counting ticket income.

### Sponsor-only stacks (no ticket dependence)

| Stack | Cash in | Enough for floor? |

**Recommended if tickets can’t be trusted:**

```text
MUST CLOSE BEFORE HARD COMMITS (Wes deposit / venue)
  1) Platinum Cognition     $10,000
  2) Gold ×1 or ×2          $6,000 each
```

### Go / no-go

## 4. What tickets should sell for

### Rule of thumb

```text
Net ticket revenue needed ÷ expected paid attendees ≈ average price
Then set early / standard / student around that average.
```

Assume:
- **Hard cap 120** (venue-dependent — confirm)
- **Sell 80–100 paid** (realistic for first year with strong names)
- **Luma ~5–8% fees** — price gross so you keep what you need
- Students / comps: **10–15** free or cheap (speakers, staff, sponsor comps)

### Ticket revenue needed (CAD)

| Goal | Need from tickets | If 90 paid | **Avg net/ticket** | **List ~ (before fees)** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover gap with Platinum $10k | ~$18k | 90 | **$200** | **~$215–230** |
| Cover all with weak sponsors | ~$25k | 90 | **$280** | **~$300** |
| Cover all, only 70 paid | ~$18k (w/ Platinum) | 70 | **$255** | **~$275** |
| Aggressive 100 paid + Platinum | ~$18k | 100 | **$180** | **~$195** |

### Recommended public tiers (CAD) — first year VanSpace

Given **Wes $5k USD + Kent first class + ~$5k venue**:

| Tier | Price CAD | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Early bird** (first 30–40) | **$149–179** | Momentum | ~2–3 weeks |
| **Standard** | **$199–249** | Default | Main revenue |
| **Late / door** | **$279–299** | FOMO | Optional |
| **Student** (ID) | **$79–99** | Cap 15–20 | Don’t let this eat the room |

**Sweet spot to publish:**

```text
Early   $169 CAD
Standard $229 CAD
Student  $89 CAD
```

**Why this band**
- Kent + Wes (+ third) is **not** a free meetup
- Space Centre is a premium room
- $229 × 80 net ≈ **$18k** before fees → with **$10k Platinum** you’re in the mid budget
- Early $169 fills the room and still respects the talent cost

### If you want cheaper tickets

You need **more sponsor cash**, not lower production:

| Ticket avg ~$99 | Need ~180 paid or huge sponsor underwrite — hard at micro cap |
| Ticket avg ~$149 | Need Platinum **and** 1–2 Golds, or 100+ sellouts |
| Ticket avg ~$229 | Platinum $10k + 80–90 tickets = viable mid |
