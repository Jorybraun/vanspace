# Kent C. Dodds — rich bio

Kent C. Dodds is a full-time software educator, engineer, and speaker based in Utah. He has spent his career making the web more approachable: through open source, courses, talks, and a growing ecosystem of learning products that now reach hundreds of thousands of developers.

He began as a JavaScript-focused engineer and represented PayPal on TC-39, the committee that standardizes the JavaScript language. Along the way he became known for testing, React, and full-stack patterns, and started sharing what he learned in public. That habit turned into a career. He has delivered more than a hundred conference talks and workshops, and built educational platforms including EpicWeb.dev, EpicReact.dev, TestingJavaScript.com, EpicProduct.engineer, EpicAI.pro, and Kody — a personal AI-assistant platform.

Kent’s open-source work is equally wide-ranging. He created React Testing Library and helped grow the Testing Library ecosystem into one of the most widely used testing tools in the JavaScript world. He is also a Remix contributor, a Cloudflare proponent, and has worked on Babel macros and many other community libraries. His contributions have earned him Google Developer Expert, Microsoft MVP, and GitHub Star recognition, as well as a GitNation OS Award for Testing Library.

As a speaker, he has appeared at React Conf, React Summit, Laracon, AgentConf, Cloudflare Connect, WeAreDevelopers, and many other events.
