| Slot | Who | Sold? |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Platinum — $10,000 (up to 2–3 slots)

### One-liner
**Underwrite the day. Take a major stage moment. Sit on the panel. Share the top line with other Platinums.**

### Job the buyer hires
> High-signal builder day we underwrite, with a real keynote-length stage moment — not a 5-minute logo read.

### What $10k includes

| Lever | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage — major moment** | **Opening**, **closing**, or agreed equivalent long block · ~30–40 min (assigned in SOW) |
| **Stage — Panel** | **Guaranteed seat** if a panel runs |
| **Proximity** | Priority activation table |
| **Brand** | “**Platinum sponsor**” line + XL logo · co-equal with other Platinums |
| **Rights** | 12–15 comps · co-announce option · full report · **category exclusivity** optional per vertical (SOW) |
| **Multi-slot** | One buyer may purchase **two** Platinum slots (e.g. open + close) |

### What Platinum is *not*
- Replacing Kent as headliner  
- Attendee email list  
- Unlimited stage time  
- The company name as the tier name on materials (“Devin package” ❌ → “Platinum” ✅)

### Why this is worth $10k
- **Keynote + panel** is the max stage package; Gold only gets fireside.  
- Production underwrite story for finance.  
- Comparable class to other high-signal Canadian builder activations.

### Selling Platinum to Devin (target, not the tier name)
Use in email: “We’re offering **Platinum** at $10k — keynote + panel + top brand line.”  
On the public site after they buy: logo + “Platinum sponsor” (company name beside the *tier*, not instead of it).

## Gold — $5,000–7,000 (1–2 slots)

### One-liner
**A fireside (or short talk) in front of the whole room — logo, table, comps.**

**Recommend publish price: $6,000 CAD** (or $5k early / $7k if stage is packed).

Public label is just a **session / talk / workshop** in the mixed lineup — not “Gold fireside” on the site.

### What Gold includes

| Lever | Deliverable |
| --- | --- |
| **Stage** | **~25–30 min session** (talk, demo, or fireside — format flexible) · hard stop |
| **Proximity** | Staffed table |
| **Brand** | Large logo · site · slides · email |
| **Rights** | **5–8 comps** · hiring blurb · report summary |

### Fireside format
- Moderator from Orbit crew or neutral community person  
- Theme locked T−14  
- ≤5 slides optional; not a feature dump  
- Hard stop enforced  

### Why not Platinum?
No Platinum Keynote, no top “Platinum sponsor” line, no category exclusivity, fewer comps.  
### Why not Silver?
**Fireside is the Gold product.**
