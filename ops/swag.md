# Swag plan — Orbit · Vancouver

Micro-con, ~80–150 people, monochrome brand, Flagship-first budget.  
**Goal:** one or two things people keep — not a tote full of landfill.

---

## 1. Principles (so you don’t overspend)

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| One **hero item** everyone wants | Five mediocre stickers-only bags |
| Match **Orbit** brand (cream / ink / mist) | Rainbow sponsor junk on the main gift |
| Size for **ticket cap + 10%** | Order for 300 “just in case” |
| Optional **sponsor add-ons** (their cost) | Promise Flagship their logo on *your* hero tee without art control |
| Useful or beautiful | Stress balls, cheap cables, mystery USB |

**Rule:** If you wouldn’t wear/use it at home, don’t print it.

---

## 2. What “usual” looks like at these events

| Event type | Typical swag |
| --- | --- |
| Free meetup | Stickers, maybe a drink ticket |
| Sponsored meetup | Sponsor stickers + sometimes socks/tee from sponsor |
| **Micro-con (you)** | **Badge + 1 hero item + stickers**; sponsors may add their own |
| Big conf | Tote + tee + booklet + sponsor haul |

You’re closer to **micro-con**: quality over quantity.

---

## 3. Recommended package (default)

### Must-have (you / event budget)

| Item | Spec | Why | Rough CAD |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Name badge** | Printable or badge + lanyard | Ops + networking | $1–3 pp or DIY print |
| **Sticker pack** | 2–3 designs, Orbit mark + wordmark, vinyl | Cheap, loved, shareable | $1–2 pp |
| **Hero item (pick one)** | See §4 | The “I was there” object | **$8–18 pp** |

### Nice-to-have (if budget or Flagship underwrites)

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Small tote or ripstop pouch | Only if fabric is good |
| Notebook + pen | Orbit cover, blank or dotted — engineers use these |
| Enamel pin | Orbit mark — higher unit cost, high keep rate |

### Skip unless free from sponsors

- Low-quality tees in one size  
- Water bottles (heavy, shipping, everyone has one)  
- Socks (hit-or-miss; size/quality issues)  
- Anything with 6 sponsor logos (looks desperate)

## 4. Hero item options (pick **one**)

| Option | Best if… | Est. unit (100–150 qty) | Lead time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. Premium tee / longshore** | You want classic conf energy | $12–20 blank+print | 3–5 weeks |
| **B. Hoodie / crew (limited)** | Flagship pays; smaller run (e.g. first 80 or staff+speakers only) | $25–45 | 4–6 weeks |
| **C. Cap / beanie** | One-size-ish, less size hell | $10–16 | 3–4 weeks |
| **D. Enamel pin + stickers only** | Budget tight, brand-forward | $4–8 pin | 3–5 weeks |
| **E. Nice notebook** | “Builder” vibe, no apparel risk | $6–12 | 2–4 weeks |

**Recommendation for Orbit v1:**

1. **Default:** stickers + **good tee** *or* **notebook** (not both unless Flagship covers apparel).  
2. **Monochrome print:** ink on cream tee, or mist print on black/bone — matches Kindolphin / brand tokens.  
3. **Art:** Orbit wordmark + small orbit mark; optional tiny “MacMillan Space Centre · [year]” on back or hem.  
4. **No sponsor logos on the hero tee** unless Flagship paid for a **co-branded** run and you approved the layout. Prefer sponsor stickers separate.

### Tee size mix (if you go apparel)

For mixed tech crowd (~100):

| Size | % | Of 100 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | 10% | 10 |
| M | 30% | 30 |
| L | 30% | 30 |
| XL | 20% | 20 |
| 2XL | 10% | 10 |

Unclaimed sizes → staff, raffle, next meetup. Collect **size on Luma** if tee is guaranteed with ticket.

## 5. Who pays for what

| Item | Paid by | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Badges, stickers, core hero | **Event** (Flagship + tickets) | In mid budget ~$800–2,000 total |
| Extra tote with only their logo | **That sponsor** | They ship to you or bring day-of |
| Co-branded Flagship tee | **Flagship** or split | Contract in SOW; art approval both sides |
| Speaker gifts | **Event** | Slightly nicer (pin, book, bottle of wine — optional) |

**Do not** put Gold/Silver logos on the main Orbit tee by default — it dilutes brand and starts logo-farm fights.  
**Do** give sponsors a **sticker square** on a shared sheet or their own sticker handout at their table.

### Budget bands (// 100 attendees)

| Tier | What’s in the bag | Total swag CAD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lean** | Badge + stickers + pin *or* notebook | **$500–900** |
| **Standard (recommended)** | Badge + stickers + tee *or* strong notebook | **$1,200–2,000** |
| **Flagship flex** | Standard + limited hoodie for speakers/staff or first-N | **+$1,000–2,000** (often Flagship-funded) |

Fits `budget.md` line “Swag / print / signage $300–1,500” for lean/standard; raise if Flagship earmarks swag.

## 6. Design system for swag

From `brand/tokens.css` / voice:

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Colors | Cream `#F4F0E8`, ink `#1A1A1A`, mist `#8B8499` — max 2 inks on apparel |
| Logo | `brand/logo-wordmark.svg` + orbit mark; keep clear space |
| Type | Quiet; no “🚀 EPIC SWAG” |
| Sponsor marks | Official lockups only (`brand/devin-assets/` for Devin); never recolor |
| Print method | Tee: screen or DTG; stickers: kiss-cut vinyl |

**Mock checklist before order:**
- [ ] Wordmark legible at chest size  
- [ ] No tiny legal text  
- [ ] Tag/print care that won’t itch  
- [ ] One colorway only for v1 (less inventory risk)

## 7. Fulfillment models

| Model | How | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Handout at check-in** | Badge + sticker + hero in a stack | Default |
| **Size pickup table** | Tees on table by size | If apparel |
| **Speaker kit** | Separate bag day-before | Speakers/mods |
| **Ship later** | Collect address — expensive, slow | Avoid for v1 |
| **Sponsor table pile** | Their swag, their problem | Gold/Flagship tables |

**Check-in flow:** ticket scan → badge → “grab sticker sheet + [hero]” → enter. Don’t block the door with size debates (pre-collect sizes or have a second volunteer).

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |

## 9. Vendors (Vancouver / online — examples, not endorsements)

| Need | Where people often go |
| --- | --- |
| Tees / hoodies | Local print shop, Printful/Printify (slow), Shirt Monkey / similar CA printers |
| Stickers | Sticker Mule, local print, Jukebox |
| Pins | Soft enamel makers (4–6 week common) |
| Badges | Canva + badge stock, or Eventbadges; or printed cards in holders |
| Notebooks | Bulk Moleskine-style or local print cover wrap |

Get **2 quotes** for the hero item. Ask: unit at 75 / 100 / 150, rush fee, color limit, setup fee.

## 10. Sponsor swag policy (put in private SOW)

```text
SWAG POLICY
- Orbit provides attendee badge + Orbit stickers + [hero item].
- Sponsors may distribute their own items at their table only.
- Sponsor logos do not appear on the official Orbit tee/hero unless
  a co-branded run is prepaid and art-approved in writing.
- No stickers on venue surfaces; no unsolicited bag-stuffing into
  bags people didn’t take.
```
