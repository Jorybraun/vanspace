# Catering — how it usually works (micro-conferences)

For VanSpace-style days (~80–150 people, ~5 hours, talks + hallway).  
**Confirm everything with the locked venue** (Space Centre *or* Science World — `ops/venue.md`). Kitchen access and alcohol rules are venue-controlled; do not assume Space Centre rules apply at Science World.

---

## 1. Who is responsible?

| Model | How it works | Common at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A. You hire a caterer** | Venue allows outside caterers; you sign with the food company | Most tech events; Space Centre often allows outside caterers — **verify Science World** |
| **B. Preferred / exclusive caterer** | Venue forces their list or in-house kitchen | Hotels, some museums |
| **C. DIY / drop-off** | Pizza, grocery trays, coffee shop delivery — you set up | Meetups, hackathons under ~60 |
| **D. Food trucks / nearby restaurants** | Guests leave or truck on site | Casual / outdoor |

**Default for ~100 pax:** **A** — one caterer (or serious drop-off for lean budget), not 12 DoorDashes day-of — **only if the venue allows it**.

**Space Centre (prior notes):** outside caterers often allowed; onsite kitchen depends on package; alcohol possible with BC rules.  
**Science World:** unknown until quote — ask preferred vendors, exclusive caterers, and public-hours constraints.

---

## 2. What people expect by event type

| Format | Food norm |
| --- | --- |
| 2h evening meetup | Pizza or snacks + drinks only |
| Half-day (morning) | Coffee + light breakfast; no full lunch |
| **Full day ~5h micro-con (you)** | **AM coffee · lunch · PM coffee/snacks** (minimum that feels “conference”) |
| Evening + drinks | Catered apps + licensed bar |

For a **ticketed day with Kent**, aim for **real lunch**, not “figure it out nearby” — unless tickets are cheap and you say so clearly on the page.

---

## 3. The usual service flow (day-of)

```text
T−14 to T−7   Final headcount to caterer (based on tickets + comps + buffer)
T−1           Confirm setup time, power, tables, dietary list
Day-of
  −90 min     Caterer load-in / coffee setup
  Doors       Coffee + light AM (pastries / fruit)
  Pre-lunch   They stage lunch out of sight
  Lunch block Clear signal: “Lunch is open” (host announcement)
  After lunch Tear-down lunch · leave water / coffee
  PM          Optional cookie / fruit / soft drinks
  End         Caterer out per venue window
```

**You** own: headcount, dietary flags from tickets, when lunch *starts*, announcing allergens.  
**Caterer** owns: food safety, staffing, serving gear (unless rentals separate).  
**Venue** owns: where food is allowed, kitchen access, alcohol policy, cleanup rules.

---

## 4. How headcount works (this confuses everyone)

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Guaranteed number** | You pay for at least this many, even if no-shows |
| **Ticket sales** | Living count — freeze guarantee ~**7–10 days** out |
| **Buffer** | Usually **+5–10%** over confirmed tickets for walk-ups / hungrier crowd / staff |
| **Comps** | Speakers + sponsors + volunteers **count** — add them explicitly |

**Example:** 90 tickets + 15 comps + 5 staff → tell caterer **110**, guarantee maybe **100** if they allow a band.

No-shows on free events are brutal (30%+). **Paid tickets** no-show less (~10–15%). Still don’t cater 1:1 to Luma “going” without a freeze date.

## 5. What’s usually on the menu (tech crowd)

**Safe, popular, not fancy:**

| Slot | Typical | Ballpark pp CAD (all-in food only, rough) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AM coffee + light | Coffee/tea + pastries or breakfast wraps | $8–15 |
| **Lunch** | Buffet or boxed: sandwiches/salads/grain bowls, or hot tray | **$22–45** |
| PM | Cookies, fruit, soft drinks, more coffee | $5–12 |
| Full day package | AM + lunch + PM | **$35–60+** pp |

**Dietary (collect on ticket form):**
- Vegetarian / vegan  
- Gluten-free  
- Nut allergy (critical)  
- Halal/kosher only if you promise it — don’t unless you can deliver  

Label everything on the table. One vegan + one GF option minimum for lunch.

**Alcohol:** optional for micro-con. Many **skip bar at daytime** tech events (cheaper, simpler, CoC). If you want beer/wine: BC rules + Smart Serve + venue OK + insurance. Often not worth it for a Saturday builder conf unless Flagship underwrites a reception.

## 6. Money: who pays

| Pattern | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Built into ticket** | Standard for paid conf — “lunch included” on Luma |
| **Sponsor underwrites lunch** | “Lunch by [Gold]” signage — nice upsell, rare at $7k unless extra |
| **Flagship underwrite** | Your $10k Flagship often **covers most F&B** in the budget story |
| **Not included** | Cheap tickets; say “lunch on your own · food trucks nearby” — only if true |

**Budget sketch (100 people, mid):**

```text
Lunch $30 pp × 100     = $3,000
AM + PM $12 pp × 100   = $1,200
Staff / service / rentals / tax / gratuity  +15–25%
────────────────────────────────
≈ $4,500–5,500 total F&B
```

Matches the mid band in `budget.md`. If Flagship is $10k and venue is ~$4k, **food is a top-3 line item** — don’t wing it.

## 7. Service style options

| Style | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buffet** | Social, flexible portions, classic conf | Lines; need space; timing |
| **Boxed lunch** | Fast, dietary labeled, no line chaos | Less “premium,” more waste |
| **Stations** | Nice feel | Cost + space |
| **Pizza drop** | Cheap | Feels meetup, not Kent-tier |

**Recommendation for Orbit:** buffet **or** high-quality boxed if room is tight. Avoid pure pizza if tickets are $50+ and Kent is on the poster.

---

## 8. Logistics people forget

- [ ] **Tables + linens** — sometimes extra rental  
- [ ] **Power** for coffee urns / chafing  
- [ ] **Water** — big jugs + cups all day (cheap goodwill)  
- [ ] **Trash / compost / recycling** — venue rules  
- [ ] **Load-in elevator / dock** — Space Centre is a real building; ask  
- [ ] **Service time vs talks** — lunch block on schedule must be real (45–60m)  
- [ ] **Sponsor tables ≠ food tables** — don’t block the buffet with banners  
- [ ] **Leftovers** — policy with caterer; sometimes donated; often not allowed to leave  

---

## 9. Ticket page copy (be honest)

**If lunch included:**  
> Ticket includes coffee and lunch. List dietary needs at checkout.

**If not:**  
> Food not included. [Nearby options / break length]. Coffee provided.

Never surprise a hungry room at 12:30.

---

## 10. What to ask Space Centre rentals (copy-paste)

```text
For catering on [date], ~[N] guests, full-day micro-conference:

1) Outside caterers allowed? Any preferred list or exclusivity?
2) Kitchen / prep access for which rooms?
3) Where can we serve (lobby vs room)? Any open-flame / reheat limits?
4) Alcohol policy and licensing requirements?
5) Load-in time, parking for caterer, waste rules
6) Any corkage / cakeage / outside food fees?
7) Can we provide our own coffee/water if lunch is catered?
```

---

## 11. Lean vs proper (decision tree)

```text
Budget tight + tickets cheap?
  → Coffee all day + quality lunch boxes OR “lunch on your own” with 75m break

Flagship closed + tickets $40+?
  → Full AM coffee + proper lunch + PM snack (default)

Unsure headcount until late?
  → Caterer with late guarantee window OR boxed lunch that scales
```

---

**Bottom line:** For your event, catering usually means **you hire an outside caterer (venue permitting), freeze a headcount ~1–2 weeks out, serve coffee on arrival + a real lunch in the midday block, and pay per person + service.** Flagship money and ticket price should cover it; don’t treat food as an afterthought if the day is five hours.
