# VanSpace — Sponsorship cold outreach playbook

You don’t need a sales background. Sponsorship is a **short, repeated process**: find the right person → open a door → send the package → book a 15‑min fit call → close.

**Assets you already have**
- Private package PDF: `ops/VanSpace-sponsor-package.pdf`
- Site: https://jorybraun.github.io/vanspace/ (or vanspace.dev when live)
- Contact: **hello@vanspace.dev**

**Tiers (for your head, not every cold email)**
- Platinum $10k — major stage moment (open mid-day *or* closing)
- Gold $6k — a session in the mix
- Silver $3k / Bronze $1.25k — optional

---

## 1. How people actually get sponsors

### Order that works

| Step | What | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Warm list first** | People who know you / owe a favor / already sponsor local events |
| 2 | **Named targets** | 10–20 companies that *should* care (DevRel, eng brand, hiring in YVR) |
| 3 | **Find the human** | Not “info@” — DevRel, partnerships, community, brand marketing |
| 4 | **Soft open** | LinkedIn note or short email — no full dump |
| 5 | **Package** | PDF after they reply or after one clear ask |
| 6 | **15‑min call** | Fit + which tier + currency + decision process |
| 7 | **SOW / invoice** | One page: deliverables, price, comps, stage slot, payment |
| 8 | **Follow-up cadence** | 3–5 touches, then polite close |

### Who buys (title hunt)

Priority order when searching LinkedIn / company site:

1. **Developer Relations / DevRel / Developer Marketing**
2. **Community / Events / Meetups**
3. **Partnerships / Alliances**
4. **Brand marketing / Demand gen** (larger cos)
5. **Founder / CEO** (startups & seed–Series B only)

**Avoid:** random sales reps, “press@”, intern DMs.

### Warm vs cold

| Warm | Cold |
| --- | --- |
| Intro from mutual | No relationship |
| They sponsored a local meetup | Never heard of VanSpace |
| You know them from work | Found via LinkedIn |

Spend **70% of time on warm** until Platinum is soft-locked.

## 2. Protocol: one target company

### A. Research (10 minutes)

Write 3 bullets before you touch them:

1. **Why them** — product fit (agents, DX, infra, hiring YVR, etc.)
2. **Why this room** — Kent opens, Wes, Space Centre, capped builders
3. **Ask** — which tier you’d offer them (Platinum if they need stage; Gold if session)

### B. Find 1–2 contacts

- LinkedIn: “DevRel [Company]” / “Developer Marketing [Company]”
- Note: email if public, or company pattern (firstname@, first.last@)

### C. First touch (pick one channel)

**Rule:** first message ≤ 5 sentences. **No price wall unless they ask.**

### D. If they engage

1. Send PDF + site link  
2. Propose **15 minutes this week** (2 time options)  
3. On the call: listen → match tier → next step (invoice / intro to budget owner)

### E. Follow-up schedule (if silence)

| When | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Day 0 | First email or LinkedIn |
| Day 3–4 | Bump: one sentence + one new fact (e.g. “Kent locked for opening”) |
| Day 10 | Short bump + PDF attached |
| Day 18 | “Closing the list Friday — still interested?” |
| After that | Park. Optional quarterly “date locked / tickets open” |

Never guilt. Always give them an easy out.

## 4. Cold call protocol (if you phone)

Most DevRel people prefer email first. Call when:

- Warm intro and they said “call me”
- Local company / known events buyer
- Email thread is stalling after 2+ opens

### Before you dial (2 min)

- Name, company, tier you’ll lead with  
- One sentence why them  
- PDF ready to email during the call  

### Call flow (max 3 minutes if cold)

| Beat | You say | Stop talking when… |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Permission | “Hi [Name], this is Jory — 30 seconds on a Vancouver builder conference, bad time?” | They say yes / no |
| 2. Hook | “VanSpace at the Space Centre. Kent C. Dodds opens. Cognition is Flagship. Looking for [Gold / supporting] sponsors.” | — |
| 3. Ask | “Open to me sending the private package, or is there a better person?” | They redirect |
| 4. Close | “I’ll email now. Can we book 15 minutes [day] or [day]?” | Calendar or “email me first” |

**If voicemail (≤20 sec):**

```text
Jory Braun — VanSpace, builder micro-conference at the MacMillan Space Centre.
Kent C. Dodds opening. Calling about sponsorship. Emailing the package —
hello@vanspace.dev. Happy to take 15 minutes if useful.
```

**Never:** read the full tier table on a cold call. Email does that work.

## 5. The 15‑minute fit call (script)

### Agenda

1. **Their goal** (2 min) — hiring, product awareness, stage, community  
2. **Event in 60 sec** — Space Centre · Kent · Wes · single track · cap  
3. **Match tier** — Platinum if they need a major moment; Gold if session  
4. **Logistics** — CAD, invoice, comps, who signs  
5. **Next step** — “I’ll send SOW + invoice draft today”

### Discovery questions

- “What does a good sponsor outcome look like for you this year?”  
- “Do you need stage time, or is brand + room access enough?”  
- “Who else has to say yes on budget?”  
- “Any category exclusivity you care about?”  

### Objection handles (short)

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| “Too expensive” | “Gold is $6k with a real session. Happy to start there.” |
| “We only do local meetups” | “This is one day, one room, named speakers — denser than six meetups.” |
| “Send something to review” | “Package is on the way. Two time options for next week?” |
| “Not this quarter” | “When does budget reopen? I’ll hold a note and check then.” |
| “We need exclusive” | “We can discuss category exclusivity in the SOW for Platinum.” |
| “Is this the Devin event?” | “It’s VanSpace. Cognition is the Flagship sponsor.” |

| Day | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Add 5 new targets + find contacts |
| Tue–Wed | 10 first touches (email/LinkedIn) |
| Thu | Follow-ups + send PDFs to anyone who replied |

### Numbers (realistic)

- **10 warm asks** → a few real conversations  
- **30 cold emails** → a handful of replies  
- **1–2 Platinum conversations** may take weeks  

You’re not failing if week one is only “sent 15 notes.” You’re building a list.

## 7. What to send when

| Situation | Send |

| They ghost after PDF | One bump with a single new fact (date, speaker, ticket interest) |

**Do not** post prices on the public site. Package stays private.

## 9. One-page “rules of the road”

1. **Right person > perfect pitch.**  
2. **Short first message.** PDF after interest.  
3. **Ask for 15 minutes**, not a commitment.  
4. **Lead with the room and speakers**, not “synergy.”  
5. **Name a tier** once you’re on a call — don’t make them guess.  
6. **Follow up 3 times**, then move on.  
7. **Write everything down** (date, what they asked, who decides).  
8. **Invoice only after slot + deliverables are clear.**
