ORDINARY LABS · COMMUNITY INITIATIVE

          
Bring the people shaping technology closer to the people building it here.

          

            
Ordinary Labs is a community initiative working to enrich Vancouver's tech scene. Each quarter, we invite an educator or builder worth learning from, invest in their visit, and create a gathering around them.

            
We are building it for public benefit, not profit, and working toward registration as a B.C. non-profit society. The goal is more shared knowledge, stronger relationships, and more reasons to come together.
