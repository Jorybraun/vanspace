BIOS SPHERE Code of Conduct

BIOS SPHERE · COMMUNITY STANDARD

A safe room for builders.

BIOS SPHERE is a day for builders, creators, and curious people. We expect attendees, speakers, sponsors, staff, and volunteers to treat each other with respect, honesty, and professionalism.

OUR COMMITMENT

Everyone deserves to participate safely.

This code applies throughout the venue, at related social events, and in event-related online spaces. Asking someone to stop is enough: respect the boundary immediately.

Report a concern Contact Jory Braun by email or speak with any staff member at the registration desk. We will respond as promptly and discreetly as we can. hello@vanspace.dev

EXPECTED CONDUCT

Respect the people sharing the room.

  1. Be respectful Welcome newcomers, disagree with ideas rather than people, and be mindful of the space you take up.
  2. No harassment We do not tolerate discrimination, intimidation, unwanted contact, or behaviour targeting age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic.
  3. Report what you experience or witness Tell Jory Braun at hello@vanspace.dev or any staff member at the registration desk. You do not need to confront anyone yourself.
  4. We act on violations We may warn, remove, or ban anyone who violates this code, without a refund, at our sole discretion.

VANSPACE · NONPROFIT INITIATIVE

Bring the people shaping technology closer to the people building it here.

VanSpace is a nonprofit initiative working to enrich Vancouver's tech scene. Each quarter, we invite an educator or builder worth learning from, invest in their visit, and create a gathering around them.

We exist to close the distance between the people shaping how software is built and the community building it in Vancouver. The goal is more shared knowledge, stronger relationships, and more reasons to come together.
