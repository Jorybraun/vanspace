Code of Conduct

A safe room for builders

BIOS SPHERE is a day for builders, creators, and curious people. We expect everyone at the event — attendees, speakers, sponsors, staff, and volunteers — to treat each other with respect, be honest, and be professional.

  • Be respectful Welcome newcomers, disagree with ideas rather than people, and be mindful of the space you take up.
  • No harassment We do not tolerate discrimination, intimidation, unwanted contact, or behavior targeting age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation, or any other personal characteristic.
  • Report it If you experience or witness a violation, contact Jory Braun at hello@vanspace.dev or any staff member at the registration desk.
  • We act on it We may warn, remove, or ban anyone who violates this code, with no refund, at our sole discretion.
