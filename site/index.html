About

One room. High signal. Zero fluff.

VanSpace is a single-track day for engineers who ship — agents, craft, and the people directing the work. Keynotes from names you already follow. A capped room so hallway conversations stick.

  • Keynotes first Kent C. Dodds, Wes Bos, and a closing speaker TBA.
  • Talks, not booth hour ~3.5h on mic inside the Space Centre package.
  • Built for builders Workshops, talks, sponsor tables, hard stops. No multi-track chaos.
