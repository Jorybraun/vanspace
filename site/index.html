What is VanSpace?

        

          VanSpace is a small event series in Vancouver. We bring builders,
          designers, engineers, and agents together for one-day micro-events with
          big energy and no fluff.
        

        

          Each event gets its own microsite and theme. The hub stays simple:
          what’s on, what happened, and how to support the next one.
