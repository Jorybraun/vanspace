## Evidence notes

### DjangoCon US 2026
URL: https://2026.djangocon.us/sponsors/information/

Official page states that sponsorship keeps ticket costs low and funds grants, speaker stipends, accessibility, meals, and care. It reports 14.1K social followers and 600K+ YouTube views. Published tiers are Diamond $20,000, Platinum $10,000, Gold $6,000, Silver $3,500, Bronze $2,300. Benefits include 1–8 sponsor tickets, tote bag insert, website link/description, break-video rotation, social thank-you, T-shirt placement at higher tiers, and “logo on all conference talk videos (available forever).” Diamond has a 10-minute speaking slot after a keynote, max 3 sponsors; sponsored workshops are separately priced at $5,000 or $12,000 depending on tier.

Caveat: larger and more established than VanSpace; YouTube/social numbers are not transferable.

### PyOhio 2026
URL: https://www.pyohio.org/2026/files/PyOhio-2026-Sponsorship-Prospectus.pdf

Official prospectus evidence from researcher: Premier $15,000 (1 available), Platinum $10,000 (2), Gold $7,500 (4), Silver $5,000, Bronze $2,500. Sponsored talks are subject to CFP review and relevance; “Content that is not relevant to the Python community will be rejected.” A coffee-break add-on is $3,000.

Caveat: volunteer regional Python conference.

### DevOpsDays Wollongong 2025
URL: https://devopsdays.org/events/2025-wollongong/sponsor/

Official page describes a 150–200-person technical audience and says sponsor support funds venue, catering, AV, travel support, and low-cost tickets. It explicitly says the event does not offer full speaker slots in return for sponsorship; sponsors may submit talks. Published package prices: Platinum AUD $12,000, Gold $8,000, Silver $5,000, Bronze $2,500, Startup $1,000. It warns that marketing may work against sponsor credibility and recommends engineers, solutions engineers, and developer advocates connecting on the community’s terms.

Caveat: one local chapter; AUD pricing.

### CppCon
URL: https://cppcon.org/sponsorship/

Official prospectus separates conference sponsorship from exhibiting and video sponsorship. It says video sponsorship has the greatest reach because people who cannot attend watch videos and videos remain online indefinitely. Benefits can include pre-roll, logo on video template, website link, video metadata link, and channel landing-page logo. It offers bespoke sponsorship options and à-la-carte community opportunities such as scholarships, game room, T-shirt, childcare, and quiet room.

Caveat: large established conference; public page does not list cash prices.

### CopyCon 2026
URL: https://www.copywritingconference.com/sponsorship/

Official page provides a strong editorial model: “Sponsorship buys association, not airtime.” Sponsor speaking is not bundled into tiers; any sponsor spotlight is ring-fenced and subject to editorial standards. It also states that audience reach should be reported honestly and that ill-fitting sponsors can damage the room. Published bands: Headline Partner £10,000–£15,000, Session Partner £3,000–£5,000, Networking Partner £3,000–£5,000, Exhibitor £1,500–£2,500. It offers student bursary and craft/award add-ons.

Caveat: online copywriting event; principles are transferable, pricing is not.

### WordCamp Canada 2026
URL: https://canada.wordcamp.org/2026/sponsor-prospectus/

Official Canadian example: Confederation CAD $8,000, Northern Lights $5,000, Maple Leaf $2,000, Coast to Coast $700. Scarcity is explicit (“One of three” and “One of six”). Benefits include 4/3/2/1 tickets, swag-bag entry, dedicated website article, and high-visibility session timer slides.

Caveat: 2025 attendance is used as proxy for 2026.

### Code Connect 2026
URL: https://cap.cloud.sap/resources/events/Code_Connect_2026_Sponsor_Packages.pdf

Official multi-event example. Series packages: €18,900 Diamond, €10,800 Platinum (only 2 available), €6,750 Gold, €4,050 Silver. It also lists lower single-day packages: €7,000, €4,000, €2,500, €1,500. Diamond receives 2 tickets per event (6 overall), and higher tiers receive multi-event visibility and swag allocations.

Caveat: three co-located events and prices exclude VAT.

### Longhorn PHP 2026
URL: https://longhornphp.com/prospectus

Official prospectus targets 150–200 attendees. Prices: Bronze $300, Silver $1,000, Gold $1,700, Platinum $2,500, Diamond $5,000. Diamond is one slot and includes a speaker slot, branded main room, and dedicated post-conference email. It supports attendee-bag inserts and closing-raffle items.

Caveat: very different cost base and event scale/pricing.

### CppCon / video and content pattern
URL: https://cppcon.org/sponsorship/

Useful direct evidence for VanSpace’s post-event promise: a mature developer conference explicitly sells video sponsorship separately from conference sponsorship because the content has a long tail and can reach people who were not in the room.

### DeveloperRelations.com
URL: https://developerrelations.com/guides/how-help-event-sponsors/

A DevRel buyer perspective says sponsors need the event date, location, audience, benefits, ROI, and a simple package grid. It specifically warns that missing dates or vague information reduces sponsor interest.

Caveat: 2017 expert article, not a current market benchmark.

## Working synthesis

- Three or four choices are easier to understand than a large tier ladder, but à-la-carte add-ons can preserve revenue without adding more tiers.
- Premium value should be scarce: one flagship/season slot, limited speaking slots, limited content or artist-designed merch sponsorships.
- Educational sponsor content should be editorially reviewed; payment should not automatically buy a keynote or control over content.
- For a 150-person event, the most defensible high-value assets are stage/content, premium post-event distribution, student access, speaker support, and community underwriting—not passive logo volume.
- A season package should be a real bundle with multi-event rights and a clear price premium or explicit negotiated scope, not “both events” accidentally included inside the same $10,000 single-event package.
- The strongest added-value concepts for VanSpace are: session/video sponsor, student access sponsor, speaker travel sponsor, artist-designed tote/tee partner, software-credit partner, and a transparent post-event report.
