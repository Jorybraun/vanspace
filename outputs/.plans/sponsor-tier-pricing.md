# Sponsor tier pricing research plan

## Key questions

1. How are sponsorship tiers commonly structured for small, premium conferences and developer events?
2. What price gaps and benefit gaps make tiers feel distinct rather than cannibalizing one another?
3. Given VanSpace's ~150-person cap, single-track format, premium speakers, no sponsor tables, and $10,000 top-tier ceiling, what tier ladder is defensible?
4. What can be added to make sponsorship valuable after the event without turning the event into a logo wall or sales channel?
5. How should a season/series package differ from a single-event package?

## Evidence needed

- Public sponsor prospectuses and rate cards from comparable developer, technology, and premium community events.
- Primary event sponsorship guidelines describing stage rights, content/editorial standards, digital distribution, swag, recruiting, and reporting.
- Current market examples of tier names, price ladders, slot scarcity, and sponsor deliverables.
- Evidence on event-content distribution and post-event value where available.
- Caveats for geography, event size, audience quality, and package negotiation.

## Scale decision

Use a broad direct-search pass plus two research subagents:

- Researcher 1: comparable developer/technology event sponsor packages and public price ladders.
- Researcher 2: sponsorship inventory, content rights, post-event value, and tier-design best practices.

Synthesis remains with the lead agent. Sources must be primary or clearly attributable and every evidence entry must include a checkable URL.
